除外したフォント · Fonts left out

These Google Fonts were tested and left out because they cannot show most kanji, so many names would fall back to another font or show boxes:

  • Aoboshi One, Cherry Bomb One, Chokokutai, Darumadrop One, Monomaniac One, Slackside One, Tsukimi Rounded (about 1,300–2,500 kanji only)
  • Shizuru, Palette Mosaic, Rock 3D, Kapakana, Yuji Hentaigana Akari / Akebono (under 1,000 kanji)
  • LXGW WenKai TC, LXGW Marker Gothic, Iansui, Huninn, Chiron GoRound TC (full coverage, but kanji are drawn in the Taiwanese style)
  • Hiragino and Yu Mincho/Gothic are Apple/Windows fonts and do not exist on the Galaxy Tab.

Italic: no Japanese web font has a true italic. The checkbox asks the browser to slant the letters itself.
