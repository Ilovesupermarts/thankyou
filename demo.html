Notes

  • The slider adjusts the track's peak gain live. You can hear the change without stopping playback.
  • Loop uses the real envelope: 3s fade-in at the head, 4s fade-out at the tail, then immediately re-arms. The clip crossfades into itself at the seam.
  • Tail 20s jumps to the last 20 seconds so you can hear the fade-out without waiting the full 17 minutes.
  • Report the peak value you land on for each clip. Those numbers become AMBIENT_TRACKS[i].peak.
