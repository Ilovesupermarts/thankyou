はじめてのヒント・デモ

Tap or swipe inside the tablet as she would. Hints 6a, 6b and 6d play one loop and move on by themselves. The others wait for her to act, and redraw after 8 seconds of no touch (twice at most).

    ＋ Keypad hint Not a step. It comes on the first time the keypad appears, for any service (a preset, or a custom one after she writes its name), whatever step she is on: a box around the keypad with 入力後、「決定」をタップ underneath it. It stays on until she keys in the first digit, then fades off (or goes when the keypad closes, if she never types). It shows the first time only.
    ＋ 6c Untick caption Not a step. It appears for the first service she ticks, whenever that happens: もう一度タップすると解除できます just below that row, for one loop. A service ticked by keying in its price counts too: the caption shows when she closes the keypad. For a custom service it appears when the writing screen closes (with the keypad hint if that is also due).
